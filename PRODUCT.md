# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Owners of family homes, small apartment buildings, and industrial/commercial
properties in Moravia (the firm's stated area since Nov 2026; seat in
Olomouc, Pavelkova 1210/10b) who need a new roof, a roof reconstruction or
modernisation, a green roof, or a timber-frame / CLT / log house built.
Secondary audience: larger construction firms looking for a subcontractor
for rafter insulation (krokevní izolace) and complex roofs. They are comparing contractors and are specifically wary of hidden
shortcuts — a leaky flat roof, skimped insulation, an unphotographed layer
that turns out to be wrong five years later. They want to understand quickly what the firm does and why
it is different, then get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A short marketing onepage that generates no-obligation inquiries
("poptávky"). It says two things and little else:

1. **TATAI does roofs and houses.** Flat, pitched and green roofs, with a
   speciality in rafter insulation and complex roofs (big firms hire TATAI
   as a subcontractor for them); a simple new-build roof is "a toy" done
   quickly at a good price; old roofs get modernised. Timber houses (stud
   frame, CLT, log).
2. **Houses are built in the firm's own hall and only assembled on site, so
   it never rains into them.** This is the differentiator and the page's
   climax (the "HALA" portal chapter). The hall also lowers costs and needs
   far fewer people than a building site, so the final price is lower:
   about the same as a brick house, ready to move in 3 months after
   groundbreaking.

Owner feedback (Oct 2026) that set this direction: cut everything a customer
does not need to decide — team size, crew counts, project counts, company
history, layer build-ups, joint details. Simple, short, no generic talk.
The long version is archived (git tag `pred-zkracenim-2026-10-21`,
texts in `docs/archiv/2026-10-21-dlouha-verze.md`).

Page order: Hero → 01 Střechy → 02 Dřevostavby → 03 Výroba (portal) → Jak to
probíhá → Časté otázky → 04 Kontakt → footer. Before adding a section, ask
whether a customer choosing a contractor would miss it; usually the answer
is no.

## Brand Personality

Precise, trustworthy, unpretentious. Short sentences, concrete facts
(10-year warranty, free site visit, hall-built houses), no adjectives doing
the work. The technical-drawing visual language (mono labels, signal-cyan
hairlines, square corners) stays — it carries the "these people are exact"
feeling so the copy doesn't have to.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI/SaaS landing page tropes: gradient text, shadow-heavy cards,
  a tiny uppercase eyebrow above every section, generic icon grids, the
  hero-metric template.
- Glossy real-estate / luxury marketing: over-polished stock photography,
  aspirational tone, anything that reads as "lifestyle" rather than "the
  work itself."

## Design Principles

1. **Short beats complete.** One sentence per card, one headline per
   chapter. If a detail only matters once the customer is already talking
   to the firm, it belongs in the conversation, not on the page.
2. **Show the hall.** Real photos from the workshop are the proof of the
   main claim; they outrank any text about it.
3. **Restraint reads as competence.** One accent color (signal cyan),
   hairline rules instead of shadows, square corners everywhere except
   pills.
4. **Every claim is true.** Only numbers the owner has confirmed (10-year
   warranty, since 2016, CLT shell within 5 days; Nov 2026: assembly one
   week incl. roof, up to three weeks incl. facade, move-in 3 months after
   groundbreaking, price ≈ brick house, new insulation of the same
   thickness insulates 2× better, area = Moravia). No invented captions,
   materials or figures. TATAI does NOT handle the project or the building
   permit — it converts an existing brick-house permit to timber.
5. **One firm, two disciplines.** Roofs and timber houses are both named
   from the very first screen. Since Nov 2026 the hero is one unified photo
   (a roof frame on a timber house), not a split diptych — owner's wish.
